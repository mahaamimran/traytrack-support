The short version: TrayTrack doesn't have a server, so there's nothing for this policy to protect you from.

TrayTrack was built to run entirely on your device. This page explains exactly what that means, in plain language.

Last updated · September 2026

The Policy

4 sections
§01

What TrayTrack stores

Your treatment plan (brand, tray count, schedule), wear-time history, day notes, appointments, and any smile-progress photos you take are saved using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData), inside your device's protected storage. That's it. There is no company server, no database, and no cloud account behind TrayTrack.

§02

What TrayTrack does not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers
  • No data ever sent over the network to us or anyone else
  • No third-party SDKs with access to your information

Everything you track stays on your iPhone, full stop.

§03

Permissions TrayTrack asks for

Camera access is used only to take a smile-progress photo — no photo is ever uploaded anywhere. Photos access, if granted, is used only to import an existing photo into your progress timeline. Calendar access, if granted, is write-only, used solely to add an appointment reminder you asked for; TrayTrack cannot read your existing calendar.

§04

Children & changes to this policy

TrayTrack is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — because it doesn't collect information at all. If this policy ever changes (for example, if an optional iCloud sync feature is introduced in the future), the update will appear on this same page with a new date above.
