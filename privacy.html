TrayTrack Privacy Policy

Nothing to collect.Your data stays yours.

TrayTrack has no server and no account. What you log lives on your iPhone, or in your own private iCloud if you choose to sync. This page explains exactly what that means, in plain English.

Effective October 12, 2026Applies to the TrayTrack iPhone app, its widgets and Live Activity

The short version

  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No server: nothing you log is sent to the developer.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Your data is on your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud only if you turn on iCloud Sync.
  • Reminders and alarms are scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.

01What TrayTrack stores, and where

To do its job, TrayTrack keeps the things you enter or create:

  • your treatment plan (aligner brand if you choose one, number of trays, days per tray, start date, daily goal), and later any refinement rounds or retainer phase;
  • your wear sessions (when your aligners or retainer came out and went back in);
  • day notes and orthodontist appointments;
  • progress photos you take or import;
  • your settings, such as reminder times and styles, appearance, theme and app icon.

All of this is saved on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage, inside storage that only TrayTrack and its own widgets can read. iOS protects it the same way it protects other apps' data.

02What TrayTrack doesn't do

  • It doesn't have an account, a login or a user ID.
  • It doesn't run or talk to any server of its own. The app contains no networking code that sends your information to the developer or anyone else.
  • It doesn't include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and doesn't use the advertising identifier.
  • It doesn't sell, rent or share your information, because the developer never receives it.

The developer has no way to see your wear history, notes, appointments or photos.

03iCloud Sync (optional, part of Premium)

TrayTrack works fully without iCloud, and iCloud Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings.

If you turn it on, your TrayTrack data (your treatment plan, wear history, notes, appointments and progress photos) is stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so it can appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

  • Your data travels between your devices and your private iCloud storage. It doesn't pass through any server run by the developer, and the developer can't see, read or access it.
  • Synced photos count toward your iCloud storage.
  • Turning sync off stops new changes from being shared. It doesn't delete what's already on your devices or in your iCloud.
  • If your Premium access ends, sync pauses. Nothing is deleted.

Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

04Camera, photos and calendar

TrayTrack only asks for these when you use the feature that needs them, and you can change your answer any time in iOS Settings.

Camera

Used to take smile-progress photos. Photos are saved inside TrayTrack on your iPhone (and in your private iCloud if sync is on). They are never uploaded to the developer.

Photo library

Used only when you choose a photo to import into your progress timeline. TrayTrack reads the photo you pick, nothing else.

Calendar

If you choose Add to Calendar for an appointment, TrayTrack asks for Calendar access so it can add that event, and later update or remove it when you change or delete the appointment. It only works with the events it added itself, and nothing from your calendar ever leaves your iPhone.

05Reminders and alarms

Switch, reinsert, evening-check and "still out?" reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. If you choose the alarm style, TrayTrack uses Apple's AlarmKit, also on your iPhone. No push service or server is involved.

06Siri, Shortcuts, widgets and Live Activity

When you use Siri or Shortcuts with TrayTrack, Apple processes your request and asks TrayTrack to perform the action (for example, logging your aligners out) or to answer a question from data already on your iPhone. Apple's handling of Siri requests is covered by Apple's own privacy policy; the developer receives nothing.

Widgets, the Control Center control and the Live Activity show your own TrayTrack data on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. They read it from your iPhone; nothing is sent anywhere. Keep in mind that Lock Screen items can be seen by anyone holding your phone.

07Purchases

TrayTrack is free to download. Premium is an optional one-time purchase made through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, restores and refunds; TrayTrack never sees your payment details. The app checks on your iPhone, using Apple's StoreKit, whether Premium is active.

08Sharing you choose

You can share a compliance report or a photo comparison, for example with your orthodontist. These files are created on your iPhone and only go where you send them through the iOS share sheet. Once shared, they are handled by the app or person you sent them to.

09Children

TrayTrack doesn't collect personal information from anyone, of any age. If a child uses the app, their data stays on their device (or in their family's iCloud if sync is on), the same as for everyone else.

10Deleting your data

Because your data never reaches the developer, you are in control of it:

  • Delete everything at once in the app: Settings → Delete All My Data. It removes your treatment, wear history, photos, notes, appointments (including the Calendar events TrayTrack added) and reminders. With iCloud Sync on, the deletion also reaches your other devices. Purchases are kept with your Apple ID.
  • Delete individual sessions, photos, notes and appointments inside the app.
  • Delete the TrayTrack app to remove all of its data from that iPhone.
  • If you used iCloud Sync, remove TrayTrack's data from iCloud in iOS Settings: tap your name, then iCloud, Manage Account Storage, TrayTrack.
  • Calendar events TrayTrack added are removed when you delete their appointment or use Delete All My Data. If you delete the app first, they stay in Calendar until you remove them there.

11Changes and contact
