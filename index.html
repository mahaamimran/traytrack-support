Frequently Asked

5 entries
N°01

Is my data private?

Yes. TrayTrack runs entirely on your device — there's no account, no login, and nothing is ever sent to a server. Your treatment plan, wear-time history, notes, and smile photos stay on your iPhone.

N°02

How do reminders work?

TrayTrack schedules local notifications for switching to your next tray and for putting your aligners back in after a meal, at times you choose in Settings → Reminders.

N°03

How do I edit my treatment plan?

Settings → Treatment lets you update your brand, tray count, current tray, schedule, and daily wear goal at any time.

N°04

How do I export a report for my orthodontist?

Settings → Share compliance report generates a wear-time summary as PDF or text that you can send directly from your phone.

N°05

Something isn't working.

Email with your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Screenshots help a lot.
